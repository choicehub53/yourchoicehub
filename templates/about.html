OUR APPROACH

Professional Jewellery & Diamond Valuation

Jewellery is more than an ornament. It can represent significant financial value, personal memories, craftsmanship and individual style.

At Your Choice Hub, our valuation approach focuses on carefully examining the important characteristics of jewellery and diamonds to help customers understand their items and their estimated value.

Depending on the item, the assessment may consider factors such as metal type and purity, weight, gemstones, diamond characteristics, workmanship and relevant market considerations.

We believe a valuation should be approached with attention to detail, professionalism and confidentiality. Every item deserves individual consideration rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

OUR VALUATION SERVICES

Understanding the Value of Your Jewellery

Our valuation services are designed to provide customers with a clearer understanding of the characteristics and estimated value of their jewellery and diamonds.

Diamond Valuation

Gold Jewellery Valuation
